FAQ

Is Rafaqat Editors AI free to use?

Yes, you can use the tools without creating an account.

Can I post the results directly?

Treat results as a strong first draft. Review and adjust them so they sound like you.

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and X.

Can it generate AI video, AI images or AI voice?

Not yet. Today you can edit photos, turn photos into a slideshow video, and hear any text read aloud in Hindi, Urdu or English. The AI Chat and writing tools work with text and can describe photos you send.

Can I download YouTube or TikTok videos here?

No. Downloading other people's videos breaks those platforms' rules and copyright. Download your own videos from YouTube Studio or TikTok's Save video option.

Is my text stored?

This site does not save your input. It is sent to the AI provider only to produce your result.

Why does a tool say the API key is missing?

The site owner has not added the AI key yet. See the README for the one-minute setup.
